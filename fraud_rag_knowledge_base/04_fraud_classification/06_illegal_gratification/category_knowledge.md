# RBI 2024 Fraud Category

## Category

Fraudulent credit facilities extended for illegal gratification.

## Source position

This category appears in the RBI 2024 fraud-reporting taxonomy. It should be selected only when the facts of a case support the category under the applicable reporting framework.

## Important boundary

The presence of a transaction alert in this project does not by itself establish that this regulatory fraud category applies. The ML alert and later investigation are separate from formal classification/reporting.
