# 04 Investigation

## Purpose

Lifecycle from detection through examination, red-flagging, investigation, classification, reporting, follow-up, resolution and closure.

## RAG role

This document is a source-grounded knowledge unit intended for semantic retrieval. It provides context for investigation and explanation. It does not replace the bank's own policies, competent investigation, or current legal/compliance review.

## Relevant terminology

detection, red-flagged, investigation, classification, reporting, resolution, closure

Flagging of Accounts (RFA) under the overall Fraud Risk Management Policy 
approved by the Board.
